# 🌟 Puneeth Rajkumar Tribute Page

A simple and responsive **Tribute Page dedicated to Puneeth Rajkumar**, one of the most beloved actors and singers in the Kannada film industry.

This project was created to showcase his life, career, achievements, and contribution to Indian cinema through a clean and visually appealing webpage.

## 📌 Features

- 🖼️ Tribute section with Puneeth Rajkumar's image
- 📖 Biography and early life
- 🎬 Highlights of his acting career
- 🏆 Awards and achievements
- 🎵 Contribution to music and playback singing
